3. Use-Case Flow Specification

Use Case: UC-01 — Submit Elective Bids

Primary Actor: Student  |  Secondary Actor: Academic Database / Prerequisite Service  |  Priority: High

Preconditions:

  • Student is authenticated and in good academic standing.
  • Elective bidding window is active/open.
  • Student has exactly 100 unspent bidding points available for the semester.

Postconditions:

  • Student's elective bids and credit allocations are persistently stored in the database.
  • 100 bidding points are marked as allocated.
  • An immutable audit log entry and confirmation receipt are generated.

Main Success Scenario

  1. Student logs into the portal and navigates to the "Elective Course Bidding" section.
  2. System displays the list of offered elective courses, descriptions, available seats, faculty, and schedule slot timings.
  3. Student selects desired elective courses and distributes exactly 100 bidding points among them based on personal priority.
  4. Student submits the bidding form by clicking "Submit Bids".
  5. System executes «include» UC-02: Validate Course Prerequisites to check the student's completed credits and course history against course prerequisites.
  6. System validates that the sum of allocated bidding points is exactly equal to 100.
  7. System confirms that no two selected electives share mutually exclusive required core slots.
  8. System commits the bid records into the active allocation pool and generates a transaction timestamp.
  9. System displays a confirmation message and summary of locked preferences.
  10. Use Case ends successfully.

Alternate Flows

  • Flow 4a: Bidding Points Total Mismatch (≠ 100 credits)
    4a1. System detects sum of credits ≠ 100 (e.g. 90 or 110).
    4a2. System blocks submission and displays: "Error: Total allocated points must equal exactly 100 credits."
    4a3. Student adjusts point distribution to 100 and resubmits.
  • Flow 5a: Unmet Course Prerequisites
    5a1. System detects student has not passed prerequisite Course P for selected Elective E.
    5a2. System rejects bid for Elective E and displays: "Ineligible: Prerequisite [P] not met."
    5a3. Student replaces Elective E with an eligible elective and resubmits.
  • Flow 7a: Potential Timetable Slot Collision («extend» UC-03)
    7a1. System detects two high-priority selected electives occupy the exact same timetable slot.
    7a2. System invokes UC-03: Resolve Timetable Conflict, alerting the student to designate preference rank.
    7a3. Flow resumes at Step 8.
  • Flow 8a: Bidding Window Closes During Submission
    8a1. System checks timestamp; bidding deadline has passed.
    8a2. System aborts transaction and notifies student that bidding is closed.
